# Free-Play Games

- Prize wheel and reels for **entertainment only**.
- You play with **play tokens you earn** from quests, daily play and events. You can't buy them, and there are no refills.
- **No Aethel Coin** goes in or comes out. Prizes are play tokens or cosmetics that stay on your account.
- **Odds are published** for every game. Every spin is logged.
- Casino-style games are 18+. Other chance games are 13+.
