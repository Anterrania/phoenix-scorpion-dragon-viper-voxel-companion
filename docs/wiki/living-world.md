# Living World

## Survival
- Alaskan, Venus and cosmic realms drain O2, warmth, food, water and sleep. Cyberpunk and the forge are softcore. Social, club and home places are off.
- Wear the right suit or stay in a powered habitat.
- Estate managers can switch survival on, off or softcore for a region.

## Getting sick
- Colds and fevers spread by contact. Rest, remedies and vaccines help. It is family-friendly.

## Creatures
- Each species has tolerance DNA that decides where it can live. Same-genus creatures can produce hybrids. Creatures only.

## Nations
- Found a fictional nation, claim estate land, set laws, hold elections, open embassies and set borders.

## Mining and crafting
- Ores sit in veins by realm and depth. Mine, refine, process, then build from blueprints.
- Blueprint sales use civic credits. Aethel Coin sales are off pending legal review.

## Land
- There are no object caps where you may build. Phones stay smooth through streaming and detail levels.
