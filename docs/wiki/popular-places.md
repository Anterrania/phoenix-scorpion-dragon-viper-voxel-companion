## Find one
1. Tap **MENU › 🧭 Teleport**.
2. Tap a place in *Travel to Landmarks*.
- Or tap **MENU › 🗺 Destinations**, then **→ go**.

Visitor counts and rankings: **Planned** (see [Economy Metrics](economy-metrics.md)).

## Events
1. Tap **MENU › 🗺 Destinations**.
2. Scroll to **📅 Events board**.
3. Tap an event, then **→ go** to teleport there.
