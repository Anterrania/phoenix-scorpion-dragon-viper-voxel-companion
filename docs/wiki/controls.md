# Controls

## Touch
- **Left half:** drag to walk.
- **Right half:** drag to look.
- **Tap** right side: dig.
- **Long-press** right side: place.
- **Two fingers:** jump.

## Fly
- Tap **FLY**.
- Hold **▲ / ▼** to go up or down.
- Let go to hover.
- Tap **LAND** to walk again.

## Keyboard (PC)
- **W A S D:** move.
- **Space:** jump.
- **E / C:** fly up / down.
- **Esc:** free the mouse.

## Menus
- Drag to scroll. It glides when you let go.
- Menus are 2 columns in portrait.
