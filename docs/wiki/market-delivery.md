# Market Delivery, Vendors & Rentals

- **Buying** puts the item in your *Received* folder right away.
- A **product's permissions** come from its items: copy only if all items copy, transfer only if all transfer.
- **Gifts** need transfer. **Redelivery** is free and never duplicates.
- **Other grids:** only items marked export. They arrive boxed and the recipient accepts.
- **Magic box:** a vendor you place on land where you can build. It always shows your live store.
- **Rent land** per minute, hour, day or week. A lease lets you in and lets you build, and access ends when it runs out. Paid rent is off for now (legal review); free and owner-granted leases work.
- **Moving from another grid:** import your inventory list. Copy/modify/transfer carry over and are never widened. Only items you made, full-perm items or export-marked items come across. Scripts are kept but need porting.
