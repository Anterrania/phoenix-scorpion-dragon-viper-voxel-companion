# Social Media

> **Partial.** The server side is built and tested headless, and it runs locally on this phone. The swipe
> feed, story viewer and editor screens are Foxy's and are coming soon.

**What you can do**
- Post text, photos (up to 10 with alt text), **clips** (short videos) and **stories** (gone after 24 h).
- React, reply, quote, repost and bookmark (into folders). Run polls, save drafts and schedule posts.
- Feeds: For you, Following, Latest, Clips, Explore, plus lists, hashtags and sounds.
- **Thumbnails load first.** In data saver, feeds show only thumbnails and clips don't autoplay; tap to
  load. Settings › Device & compatibility › Battery & data.
- Pages and events (with RSVP).
- Context notes: helpful notes show once people who don't usually agree have rated them helpful.

**Your privacy**
- Private account, close friends, mute, restrict, block and hidden words.
- "Less of this topic" for 1, 3 or 7 days.
- Photos have their location (GPS) and camera data removed before they are saved.

**Safety**
- Report any post, account or message. Three reports hide a post until a moderator looks.
- Mature posts are only shown to adults who opted in.
- Companions can't post on their own. The owner approves every companion post.
