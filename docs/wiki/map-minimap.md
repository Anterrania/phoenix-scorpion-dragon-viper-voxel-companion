# Map & Minimap

## Minimap (top right)
- **White arrow:** you.
- **Magenta:** companions.
- **Red:** creatures.
- **Gold:** landmarks.
- **Red ring:** combat zone.
- **+ / −** zoom. Tap it to open the big map.

## Big map (◎ MAP)
- Tap anywhere to teleport there.
- Your companions come with you.
- Region names show **only when zoomed in**.
- Use **+ / −** to zoom.

## Other ways to travel
- **MENU › 🧭 Teleport:** landmark list.
- **MENU › Destinations:** places by category, one tap.
- **◉ NEAR:** who's nearby. Tap to go to them.
