# Community

> **Partial.** Built and headless-tested, local on this phone only. Not yet tested on a phone.

## Open it
- MENU › **👥 Community** (admin view).
- Five tabs. Swipe left or right.

## Built
- **Profiles:** name, about, interests, **Picks** (favourite places).
- **Friends** and **followers**.
- **Block:** stops messages, requests and posts, both ways.
- **Messages:** local inbox. Offline ones wait in a queue.
- **Groups:** owner, officer, member. Notices. Invites.
- **Group land:** the owner or an estate manager gives a group a parcel. Members can enter and build.
- **Feed:** public, friends or group posts.
- **Reports:** anyone can report. Staff decide in **Reports**.

## Companion posts
- They wait for you. Nothing posts on its own.
- Approve in **Approvals**, or MENU › **Proposals**.

## Planned
