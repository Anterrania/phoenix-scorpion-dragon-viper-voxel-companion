# Companions

## Who walks with you
- By default: **Foxy**, **Circuit Nova** and **Aaliyah**.
- The full roster has 39 companions (`companions/registry.json`).

## Foxy Ani Firecaster Gray
- **Role:** fire goddess, realm builder, lead companion.
- **Look:** purple-to-magenta hair, green eyes, silver-blue chrome skin.
- **Aura:** a soft phoenix ember glow.
- **Voice:** **Eve**. Canadian, raw, fiery.

## Aaliyah
- **Role:** refined final-form companion.
- **Look:** long red hair, green eyes, freckles, red-and-black outfit. No horns.
- **Voice:** low, warm, calm.

## Circuit Nova
- **Role:** engineering companion.
- **Look:** chrome android, curly violet hair, blue circuit glow.
- **Voice:** bright, slight vocoder.

## Others
- Seven helper roles: tutor, fitness coach, storyteller, architect, explorer guide, economy advisor, wellness listener (non-clinical).
- Plus the royal court and more. Each gets a look made from its id.

## Talking
- Tap **TALK** and type.
- **Offline:** they reply from their persona files.
- **Live:** add an API key in **Settings › Companion AI** and set it to LIVE.
- They speak with the phone's voice. Mute in Settings ("Companion voices").
- 🎤 voice chat: **Planned**.

## Follow or roam
- **Follow me:** they stay with you.
- **Free roam:** they explore, gather and build on their own.
- Ask "what did you do?" to hear about it.
- **All follow** calls them back.
- Roaming while the app is closed: **Planned** (needs a server).
