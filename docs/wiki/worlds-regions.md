## Get around
- **MENU › 🧭 Teleport** opens *Travel to Landmarks*.
- **MENU › ◎ World Map**, or radial › **Map**.
- **MENU › 🗺 Destinations** opens the *Destination Guide*.

## Regions & parcels
- **MENU › 🏛 Region / Estate** shows *Region: … · Parcel …*.
- Land tiers (parcel, region, homestead, planet) are **Planned**. See [Aethel Coin Exchange](aethel-coin-exchange.md).
