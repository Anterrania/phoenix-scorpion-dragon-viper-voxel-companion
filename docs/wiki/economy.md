# Economy

> **Mostly Planned.** Real money needs legal review first.

## Aethel Coin (AEC)
- The in-game money, like L$.
- **Built:** a local wallet on your phone.
- Game-only. No cash-out. Not crypto.

## Marketplace
- **Built (stub):** "List on Marketplace" saves a local draft.
- **Planned:** web + in-app store, vendors, reviews, delivery to your Satchel.
- **Planned:** creator permissions (copy / modify / transfer).
- 18+ items hide behind the age gate.

## Aethel Exchange
- **Planned:** buy/sell AEC between players.
- **⚠ Needs legal and compliance review** before launch.
- Until then, AEC stays a closed game currency.
- Details: [Aethel Coin Exchange](aethel-coin-exchange.md) · [Economy Metrics](economy-metrics.md)

## Fees
- Upload and land fees: **Planned**. Prices are placeholders.
