# Side Project: Rap Music Video Concept, starring Aaliyah (companion)

> Idea doc only. Aaliyah here is the in-game **original companion character**, not the late singer. Choreography is *inspired by* the general dance styles of Aaliyah, Ciara and Michael Jackson; no copying of specific routines, likeness or songs. Lyrics are not written yet and will be original.

- **Runtime:** ~8 min, in 8 sections (verse/chorus pairs), about 60 s each.
- **Engine:** captured in-game (Godot cinematic camera + the companion dance emotes), with possible extra rendering.
- **Cast:** Aaliyah (lead), Foxy Ani Firecaster Gray (fire feature), Circuit Nova (beat/hologram feature).

| # | Section | Realm / set | Movement style inspiration | Visual beats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro verse | Purple-velvet lounge home base | Smooth 90s R&B groove, slow isolations | Candle-glow, close-ups, red hair flowing |
| 2 | Chorus 1 | Rooftop, cyberpunk city | Sharp hits + floorwork (Ciara-inspired energy) | Neon rain, crew of voxel dancers |
| 3 | Verse 2 | Alaskan glacier at aurora | Sleek, fluid tutting / arm waves | Breath fog, aurora ribbons |
| 4 | Chorus 2 | Ice cave | Lean-in group formation | Light refracts through ice blocks |
| 5 | Verse 3 (Foxy Ani feature) | Venus caldera | Theatrical fire-casting choreography | Lava sparks, phoenix burst |
| 6 | Chorus 3 | Cosmic crystal field | Moonwalk-style glides, spins, freeze poses (MJ-inspired) | Zero-g stardust |
| 7 | Verse 4 (Circuit Nova feature) | Circuit hologram stage | Robotic popping/locking | Circuit lines pulse to the beat |
| 8 | Final chorus / outro | All realms montage → home base | Full ensemble, callback moves | Realms build themselves around the dancers |

**Production needs:** a dance-emote animation set (mocap or keyframed), a cinematic camera tool, beat-sync markers, original beat + lyrics (to commission or write), and rights cleared for everything. The release is shared through Social Hooks with user approval only.
