# Getting Started

## Install
- Download the APK on your phone.
- Tap it to install.
- Android may ask to allow installs from this source. Say yes.

## Update
- Install the new APK **over** the old one.
- No uninstall needed.
- Your avatar, wardrobe and settings stay.
- If it says "app not installed", see the [FAQ](../FAQ.md) (signing mismatch).
- In-app "update available" button: **Planned**.

## First launch
- You land on **Welcome Island** (tutorial). See [Welcome Island](welcome-island.md).
- You make your avatar. See [Avatars & DNA](avatars-dna.md).

## Owner code (Phoenix only)
- Open **MENU › My Avatar & Profile**.
- Enter your private owner code. Tap **Unlock**.
- This unlocks your Sovereign look, Monarch outfits, Proposals and History.
- It's a local lock on this phone. It's not real security yet.
- Server accounts: **Planned**.

## Portrait mode
- The app is designed for portrait (720×1280).
- Auto-rotate still works if you want landscape.

## Exit and background
- **EXIT** and **BG** sit under the minimap. They are always visible.
- **BG** puts the app in the background.
- Android **Back** closes the open panel, then backgrounds the app.
