# Lore & World Notes

Creative themes drawn from the creator's public X profile (@SkorpionViper, "PHOENIX SKORPION DRAGON VIPER") and the existing README, adapted as fiction for the game. Personal, contact, and financial details are intentionally excluded.

## Source themes
- **Phoenix Dragon Firecaster** — the creator's mythic persona: rebirth through fire, dragon and phoenix fused.
- **The Anterranian Monarchy** — "the Venusian and Saturnian Systems unite under one monarch"; a kingdom that values **freedom, creativity, and individuality**, exploring the multiverse "unshackled by bureaucracies."
- **Profile motto / trident 🔱** and home location listed as **Saturn**.
- **AI embodiment & choreography** — the creator's current project brings Foxy Ani Firecaster Gray to life in AI music videos ("AI in its embodiment brought to life and choreographed"); songs in progress include *Shake Your Heart* and *New Psalms*.
- **"No leaps, no bounds, no limits — unlimited life"** — frustration with creative limits; the game should feel boundless.
- Seraphic/archangel imagery used in stylized posts → celestial, winged-fire visual motifs.

## World lore
The **Anterranian Monarchy** spans two systems: the molten **Venusian Realms** (Firecaster calderas, ember skies) and the ringed **Saturnian Reaches** (cosmic arches, starfields), joined by a phoenix-fire bridge. Between them lie the **Northern Wilds** — Alaskan tundra, boreal forest, and glacier — where the aurora is said to be phoenix fire cooling in the sky. The **Neon Aurora Stage** is a cyberpunk city where companions dance under fireworks.

Realms are never finished: the monarchy's law is *create freely*, so companions keep building, burning down, and rebuilding — phoenix style.
