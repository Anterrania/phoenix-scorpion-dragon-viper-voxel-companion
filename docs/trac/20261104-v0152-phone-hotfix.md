# v0.15.2 phone hotfix (owner phone test of v0.15.1 + social v0.41)

- ONE-LOGIN-2 (root cause): ui_root checked "vault sealed?" in _ready, BEFORE Profile's deferred restore of the
  remembered owner session ran, so the PIN pad opened on top of the social sign-in -> two logins. Now
  `_launch_unlock()` decides at deferred time: restored session -> no prompt; social menu present -> its sign-in is
  the only one; no social menu -> PIN pad.
- SCROLL-2: 14 screens used a plain ScrollContainer, which cannot be dragged when the finger lands on a button.
  All are KineticScroll now; only the top-most list under the finger scrolls.
- PHONE-3: landscape Godot panels are a right side sheet (<=46% width); WebView textZoom follows the system font
  scale capped at 115% (large font scale blew the social menu up).
- PHONE-4: social v0.42 bundle: Gate scrolls / splits in landscape, login form centered, focused field above keyboard.
- Tests: tests/v0152_tests.gd (launch login paths, finger scroll everywhere, 2400x1080 and 1080x2400 layout);
  SkorpionViper scripts/e2e/phone-sizes.mjs (412x915 portrait, landscape, keyboard, 130% font).
- Known: headless SecureFile "integrity check failed" lines are pre-existing test-env noise (43 before, 49 now).
