# Restrictions

> **Partial.** Off unless you turn it on. Adults only.

Open **MENU**, then **⚖ Land & Safety**, then **Limits**.

- **I'm 18 or older. Turn on** lets people you pick switch off a few of your own abilities for a while,
  like flying or teleporting to landmarks.
- **Who may restrict you:** add or revoke people. Revoking ends their restrictions right away.
- **Safeword: clear all now** always works.
- Each restriction ends by itself within 4 hours.
- Nobody can ever force you to move, sit, change clothes or talk.
- The emergency stop pauses everything. Only you, unlocked, can turn it on for a companion.
