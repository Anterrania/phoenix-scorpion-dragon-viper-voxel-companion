# Frontier

> **Partial.** The systems are built and headless-tested, but not yet tested on a phone. The flight HUD, star map routes, wrist hub and ship building are Foxy's and are coming. Concepts are inspired by classic space-sim games. It's all our own code, names and art.

**Open it:** MENU → **🪐 Frontier**. Swipe between five tabs: **Home** (overview + organisations) · **Trade** (market + contracts) · **Industry** · **Holdings** (land + ships) · **Comfort**.

## What each section does
- **Overview:** your tier, the planets, the shard and server, and activity counts.
- **Market:** buy and sell orders per item and place. A 1% fee applies when an order waits on the book, and a 2% sales tax is paid by the seller. Both are burned.
- **Contracts:** trades, deliveries with collateral, auctions, help requests, and staff missions that raise your reputation with a faction.
- **Industry:** recipes, blueprints (originals and limited copies), material and time research, and job slots by tier. Jobs finish while the app is closed. Tap **Collect finished jobs**.
- **Orgs:** groups from Community are organisations. The owner makes titles (bundles of rights), shares land rights by tag, and forms alliances.
- **Land:** claim a parcel in a region the owner opened for claims. Renew every 30 days, with 7 days of grace after that. Claims can only be contested on land marked for damage (combat).
- **Ships:** register ships, park them in a hangar or fly them, and insure them. A claim replaces a lost hull after a short timer.
- **Comfort:** field of view, turn vignette, snap turn, camera shake, head bob, motion blur, level horizon, and control layouts (default, left-handed, one thumb). **Calm preset** sets everything to the gentlest options.

## Who can do what
- **Guest:** look only.
- **Resident:** trade, contracts, industry, own and insure ships (basic plan).
- **Premium / Creator:** claim land, the Plus insurance plan, more job slots.
- **Builder / Staff:** post missions, the Fleet insurance plan, record ship losses.
- **Owner:** everything, including shard settings (needs the owner unlock).

## LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Prices, rewards, upkeep and insurance are **Aethel Coin numbers only**. No coin moves between wallets until the economy and the legal review are done.
