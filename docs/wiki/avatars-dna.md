# Avatars & DNA

## Create your avatar
- First launch opens **Create Your Avatar**.
- Pick name, body, skin, hair, streaks, eyes, outfit, glow, trim and extras.
- Change it anytime: **MENU › My Avatar & Profile**.

## DNA blend
- Pick 2–3 parent looks: Ember, Frost, Nova, Titan, Sylph.
- Slide how much of each.
- Fine-tune height, build, shoulders, head, jaw, legs, arms.

## Attachments
- Halo, crown, wings, cape, scepter on attach points.
- **Alpha masks** hide body parts under clothes.

## Your own 3D items
- Put a `.glb` in the phone's Download folder.
- Tap **Import .glb wearable**.
- Newer Android may block this. A file picker is **Planned**.

## Save and share
- **Export avatar file** saves your whole look.
- Clothes live in the **BAG** (wardrobe).

## Planned

- Baked clothing layers.
