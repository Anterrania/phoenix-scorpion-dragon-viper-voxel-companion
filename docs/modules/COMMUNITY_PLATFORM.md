# Community Platform (Dolphin-style concepts)

> **Planned module, not implemented. No live servers or services exist.** Phase: 9+.

Concepts inspired by community/social-network platforms like Boonex Dolphin (profiles, groups, events, forums, media). Original implementation; no Dolphin code. Hosting platform still to be chosen.
- **Profiles:** player handle, avatar, showcased realms and companions (private by default).
- **Groups/Clans:** shared realms and group chat.
- **Events:** build contests, music-video premieres, realm tours.
- **Forums/Feeds:** build showcases and guides, with moderation queues.
- **Media gallery:** screenshots and clips, uploaded only by user action.
- **Moderation and safety:** reporting, blocking, age-appropriate content rules, and companion personas never exposed to strangers without the owner's opt-in.
- Needs backend, legal, privacy and moderation work before anything goes live. Coordinate with the Business Setup and Law bots.
