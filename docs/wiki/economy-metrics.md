# Region Status & Economy Metrics

> **Planned. Concept only.** Nothing here is measured yet. There are **no real numbers**.

## Region status (planned)
- Online / offline, visitors now, and owner.
- Needs server regions first (VIPER-8).

## GDP-style metrics (planned)
- **Region output:** total AEC spent on sales, land fees and upload fees in a region.
- **Trade volume:** AEC traded on the exchange per day.
- **Money supply:** AEC in all wallets.
- **Sinks:** fees removed from circulation (the exchange fee is burned).
- **Market rate:** AEC price over time (OHLC chart).

## What exists now
- One local wallet on your phone. No server totals.
- Exchange matching scaffold, not connected to the app. See [Aethel Coin Exchange](aethel-coin-exchange.md).

⚠ The currency and exchange need **legal review** before going live.
