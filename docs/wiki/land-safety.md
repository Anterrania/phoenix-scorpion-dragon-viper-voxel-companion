# Land & Safety

> **Built** and headless-tested. Not tried on a phone yet.

Open **MENU**, then **⚖ Land & Safety**. Swipe between the tabs.

## Land
- Name, description and category of the parcel you stand on.
- **Teleports:** anywhere, landing point, or blocked. **Set landing point here.**
- Media and music links. Only links that start with https:// or http:// work.
- **Estate managers:** only you can add or remove them, up to 15. Companions can't be managers.
- **Message the estate.**

## Access
- Who can come in. Adults-only option.
- **Allow** and **Ban**. Bans can last always, 1 h, 24 h or 7 days.

## Objects
- Land impact for the owner, the group and others.
- **Auto-return** others' objects after a set time.
- **Return** by owner. Returned objects go to that owner's Lost and Found. Nothing is deleted.

## Who can edit
You (unlocked), estate managers, and the parcel's owner. Everyone else can only look.
