# Game Balance & Inspirations

> **Future module, not implemented.** Phase: Ongoing (Phase 6+). Design notes only.

| Inspiration | Takeaway |
|---|---|
| Star Citizen | First-person immersion, mobiGlas UI, DNA faces, survival stats |
| Dual Universe | Voxel building of ships and bases at scale, player-made economy |
| X series (X4) | Living economy, station building, faction sims |
| EVE Online | Player-driven markets, travel risk vs. reward |
| Second Life | User creation, scripting, social spaces, avatars |
| The Sims / SimCity | Needs, build/buy, city zoning |
- **Pillars:** creative freedom first (creative mode skips needs and costs); survival and economy are opt-in layers.
- **Pacing:** companions build fast enough to feel helpful without replacing the player's own creativity.
- **Mobile limits:** budget the edits per second, view distance and companion count (start at 3).
- Track the tuning numbers in `data/balance.json` (future).
