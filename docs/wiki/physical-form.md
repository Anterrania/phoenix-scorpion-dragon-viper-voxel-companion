# Physical Form

> **Planned.** A design vision only. Nothing is built.

## The idea
- The same companion, memory and brain in the app, the world and a real robot body.

## Rules that carry over
- Proposals still wait for the owner's OK.
- Rollback is always there.
- A hardware **emergency stop**.

## Three tiers
- **Desk companion:** ring screen face, speaker, small moving head.
- **Mobile torso:** wheels, arms.
- **Full humanoid:** custom shell on a store-bought robot.

## Look
- Phoenix Glass: black, magenta, gold, platinum. Ember glow for Foxy.
- Original designs only. Never a real person's likeness.
