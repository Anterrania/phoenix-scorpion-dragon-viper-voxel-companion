# How-To Guides

Exact button names. Radial = the hub on the right edge (Admin · Security · Chat · Map · Proposals · Menu).

## Teleport to a place
1. **MENU › 🧭 Teleport**.
2. Tap a landmark, or **🏠 Spawn (Alaskan Aurora Tundra)**.

## Open the map
- Radial › **Map**, or **MENU › ◎ World Map**.
- Search with *Search landmarks…*.

## Change camera
1. **MENU › ⚙ Settings**.
2. Tap **Switch 1st / 3rd person**.

## Change your look
1. **MENU › 🧑 Avatar** or **👜 Wardrobe**.
2. Pick an outfit. Wardrobe also shows your *Satchel*.

## List an item
1. **MENU › 👜 Wardrobe**.
2. In *Satchel*, tap **List on Marketplace**. Saves a local draft.

## Ask for help
1. **MENU › ❓ Help**.
2. Type in *Ask a question (e.g. how do I fly?)*, then tap **Ask**.

## Terraform
1. **MENU › ⛰ Land Tools**.
2. Set *Brush size* and *Strength*.
3. Hold **APPLY (hold to keep sculpting)**.
- If it says *Terraform not allowed in this region.*, change it in Region / Estate.

## Region settings
1. **MENU › 🏛 Region / Estate**.
2. Use **Who can enter**, **Who can terraform**, **Land texture**.

## Lock the owner session
1. Radial › **Security**.
2. Tap **Lock now**. See [Owner Lock](owner-lock.md).

## Run in background / quit
- **MENU › ⤓ Background** or **✕ Exit app**.
