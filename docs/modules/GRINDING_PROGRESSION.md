# Grinding & Progression — PLAN
- **Trade skills (New World-style):** gathering (mining, logging, harvesting, skinning, fishing), refining (smelting, weaving, tanning), and crafting (weaponsmith, engineering, cooking, arcana). Each skill goes to level 200. Refined goods feed the economy (ECONOMY_BANKING.md).
- **Skill-by-use (Skyrim-style):** doing an action earns XP in that skill (`scripts/progression/skill_by_use.gd`). XP per level goes up on a curve. Character level comes from total skill-ups. Companions learn skills too, and DNA traits change their learning rate.
- **Pets / heroes / gems (League of Angels-style):** collectible companion pets and hero forms with star tiers, gem sockets (fire/circuit/grace), and awakening. **No paid gacha.** Everything is earned in-game unless the Law Bot reviews a monetization plan.
- Anti-grind guardrails: daily diminishing returns, catch-up XP, offline progress capped at 8h.
