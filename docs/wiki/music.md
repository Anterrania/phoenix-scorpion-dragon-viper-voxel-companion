# Music

## Default music
- Phoenix's SoundCloud (**Anterranian Monarch**) plays everywhere by default.
- Playlist: **Cosmic Series**. Celestial Convergence, Dragon of the Cosmos, God's Palace, Venusian Dreams, Throne Room.

## Controls
- **MENU › ♪ Music.**
- Shows the song. **Prev / Next.**
- **🔇** mutes. Mute is remembered.
- **▶ Play on SoundCloud** opens the SoundCloud app.
- Paste **your own** SoundCloud link there.

## Parcel music
- A parcel's music URL overrides the default while you're there.

## Why it opens the SoundCloud app
- In-game streaming needs a SoundCloud API key.
- Without it, songs play in the SoundCloud app.
- A royalty-free fallback track is built in.

## Phoenix Radio
- A radio near spawn, with the Radio Club dance floor.
