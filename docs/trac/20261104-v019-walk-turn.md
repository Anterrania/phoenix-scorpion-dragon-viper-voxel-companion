# v0.19.0 (versionCode 25) — Second Life-style walking and turning

| ID | Change |
|----|--------|
| WALK-1 | Touch joystick in third person walks relative to the camera. The avatar turns to face the walk direction at a capped rate (7 rad/s) instead of strafing. |
| WALK-2 | Keyboard in third person works like SL: W/S walk along the facing and A/D turn (2.4 rad/s). |
| WALK-3 | The camera keeps its view while the body turns, then eases back behind (v0.18 snap back). |
| WALK-4 | Turning on the spot plays a small walking step instead of sliding. |
| WALK-5 | Companions turn toward where they walk at the same capped rate. When standing, they only turn to face you once you are more than 60° off to the side, then turn all the way (hysteresis, like SL avatars). |
| WALK-6 | Mouselook (first person) still strafes as before. |
| TEST | tests/v019_tests.gd: math, player joystick/keyboard/mouselook, companion wiring. |
