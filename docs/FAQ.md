# FAQ

Short answers. More detail is in the [Wiki](wiki/INDEX.md).

## Getting started

**How do I install it?**
Download the APK on your phone and tap it. Allow installs from that source if Android asks.

**How do I update?**
Install the new APK over the old one. Don't uninstall. Your stuff stays.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. The world, avatar and offline companion replies all work. Live AI and SoundCloud need internet.

**Can I play in portrait?**
Yes. Portrait is the default. Landscape works too.

**How do I close the app?**
Tap **EXIT** under the minimap.

**How do I put it in the background?**
Tap **BG**, or press Android Back until it backgrounds.

**The tutorial island again?**
**MENU › Welcome Island**. You can skip it or go back anytime.

## Playing

**How do I move?**
Drag the left side to walk. Drag the right side to look.

**How do I fly?**
Tap **FLY**. Hold ▲ or ▼. Tap **LAND** to walk.

**How do I teleport?**
Open **◎ MAP** and tap a spot. Or use **MENU › 🧭 Teleport**.

**Why are there no names floating over people?**
They're off to cut clutter. Turn them on in Settings.

**Why can't I see region names on the map?**
They only show when you zoom in.

**Where can I fight?**
Only in the **Ember Arena** in Venus. Everywhere else is safe.

**How do I breed Wyrmlings?**
Feed two of them at the Wyrmling Nest, then breed the fed pair.

**Can I wear my own 3D items?**
Yes. Put a `.glb` in Download and tap **Import .glb wearable**.

## Companions

**Who are my companions?**
Foxy, Circuit Nova and Aaliyah walk with you by default.

**What voice does Foxy use?**
**Eve**. Canadian, raw, fiery.

**How do I talk to them?**
Tap **TALK** and type.

**Can I talk by voice?**
Not yet. Voice chat is **Planned**.

**Do companions keep living when the app is closed?**
Not yet. That needs the server (**Planned**).

**Can they learn?**
Yes. Teach them with "when I say X, reply Y". The owner approves what sticks.

## Owner (Phoenix)

**How do I unlock owner powers?**
**MENU › My Avatar & Profile**, enter your owner code and tap **Unlock**.

**Where do I approve what companions learned?**
**MENU › 📜 Proposals**. Tap Approve or Reject.

**Can I undo a change?**
Yes. Tap **Roll back** on a learned cell in Proposals.

**Where's a companion's memory?**
**MENU › 🕮 History**. Pick a companion and search.

**How do I back up a companion?**
In History, tap **Export**. Import merges it back.

**How do I change the land?**
**MENU › ⛰ Land Tools**: raise, lower, flatten, smooth, noise, revert.

**Can I set who enters my land?**
Yes, in the Region / Estate panel. It's enforced once the server exists.

**Is Aethel Coin real money?**
No. It's game-only for now. Cash-out and the exchange need legal review first.

## Troubleshooting

**The download stalls.**
Stay on Wi-Fi and keep the screen on. Delete the half file in Downloads, then try again.

**"App not installed" when I update.**
The new APK was signed with a different key than yours. Android blocks that. Use builds from the same source as your current one. If you must switch, back up first (History › Export), then uninstall and install.

**It says CHAT OFFLINE.**
Live chat needs an API key. Add it in **Settings › Companion AI** and set LIVE. Offline replies still work.

**No music plays.**
Check that **🔇** isn't on. Check your internet. Without a SoundCloud API key, songs open in the SoundCloud app instead.

**A parcel plays different music.**
That parcel has its own music URL. Leave it to hear the default.

**My .glb import failed.**
Newer Android can block reading Download. A file picker is **Planned**.

**The game is slow or hot.**
Tap **GFX** to drop to **LOW**.

**Proposals or History won't open for me.**
They're owner-only. Enter the owner code first.

**Where do I give feedback?**
Tell Foxy in chat. Gold **★ YOUR INPUT HERE** signs mark open decisions.
