# Combat, RP & Breedables

## Combat
- Only in the **Ember Arena** (Venus). Red ring + sign.
- Everywhere else is safe. HUD shows ⚔ or 🛡.
- Health plus a regenerating shield.
- **⚔ swipe** (melee) and **🔥 fire bolt** (magic).
- Three **Cinder Stalkers** patrol. They respawn after 15 s.
- If you die, you respawn at spawn, fully healed.

## Roleplay
- **🎭 RP:** character sheet.
- RP titler: name, title, faction, IC/OOC/AFK.
- IC / OOC switch in TALK.
- **/me** emotes.
- Poses: wave, bow, sit, dance.

## Breedables
- **Ember Wyrmlings** live in the Wyrmling Nest near spawn.
- Feed them. Breed a fed pair (cooldown applies).
- Babies inherit color, horn and wing genes.
- 2% mutation chance.
- Saved on the phone.
