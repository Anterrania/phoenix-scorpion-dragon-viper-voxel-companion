# Tiers

> **Built** (headless-tested). Not yet tested on a phone. Paid membership is planned.

## The six tiers
1. **Guest:** browse, report.
2. **Resident:** post, message, friends, join groups. Build where the land allows.
3. **Premium / Creator:** albums, classifieds, uploads, marketplace. Create groups, events, polls. **Broadcast** streams and parcel media where they have media rights on the parcel (see Media, Voice & Broadcast).
4. **Builder / Staff:** bots and employees. Build tools on staff land. Moderate.
5. **Estate Manager:** region and estate settings. Gives groups land.
6. **Owner:** everything. Needs your owner unlock.

## Land rules
- Bans always win.
- **Build off** on a parcel: only you.
- Terraform **everyone:** residents and up.
- Terraform **group:** that group's members, plus staff.
- Terraform **owner only:** you, staff and estate managers.

## Safety
- Locked owner session = Guest.
- E-stop: bots can only browse.
- Nobody else can become Owner.

## Change a tier
1. MENU › **👥 Community** › **Roles**.
2. Pick a tier next to a member.
3. Tap **Save roles**. Owner only.
