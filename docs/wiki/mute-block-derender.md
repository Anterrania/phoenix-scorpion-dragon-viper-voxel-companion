# Mute, Block & Derender

> **Built.** Works for everyone, even guests. Your lists are private and stay on your phone, encrypted.

Open **MENU**, then **⚖ Land & Safety**, then **Safety**.

- **Mute:** you stop seeing or hearing them. They aren't told.
- **Block:** mutes everything, and also unfriends, unfollows and stops messages.
- **Derender:** hides an avatar or object for you only. **Saved** ones stay hidden. Temporary ones come
  back after a teleport, unless you switch **Show temporary after teleport** off.
- **Show** brings a hidden one back. **Clear temporary** brings all temporary ones back.
