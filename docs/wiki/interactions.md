# Interactions

Every button below has this exact name in the app.

## Talk to a companion
1. Tap radial › **Chat**, or **MENU › 💬 Talk**.
2. Type in *Type to your companion…*.
3. Tap **Send**.
- Voice: hold **🎤 Hold** and speak.
- **IC** switches in-character / out-of-character.

## Follow or roam
1. Open **💬 Talk**.
2. Tap **Mode: Follow me** to switch one companion.
3. Or tap **All follow** / **All roam**.

## Roleplay sheet
1. **MENU › 🎭 Roleplay** opens *🎭 Character Sheet & Titler*.
2. Fill in *Name:* and **Status:**.
3. Tap **💾 Save sheet & update titler**.

## Wyrmlings
1. **MENU › 🐉 Wyrmlings** opens *🐉 Ember Wyrmlings*.
2. Tap **🍖 Feed**.
3. Select two, then **💞 Breed selected pair**.

## Approve what companions learn
1. Radial › **Proposals**, or **MENU › 📜 Proposals**.
2. Review each one. Owner only. See [Brain, Memory & Proposals](brain-memory-proposals.md).

## Stop all companions
1. Radial › **Security**.
2. Tap **■  STOP ALL COMPANIONS** (anyone can).
3. To resume: **Hold to resume companions** (owner).
