Owner: **Foxy**. Concepts from idle MMOs, RPGs and open-world games, our own rules.

- Pets and breedables (`scripts/breedables`), with bonding and care.
- Mounts: summon, ride, stamina, bonding level.
- Idle/offline progress for teams (gather while away) is dev SBX-15. It never sells boosts.
