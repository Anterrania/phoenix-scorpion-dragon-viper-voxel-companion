# Quests & Missions

- **Quests** have objectives: collect, visit, talk, use, build, deliver, defeat, photo. Some must be done in order, and some are optional.
- **Missions** are timed. They fail if time runs out or you leave the region.
- Some quests unlock after others, and finishing one can offer a choice of next quests.
- **Daily** quests come back after a cooldown.
- Rewards: XP and free play tokens. No Aethel Coin rewards (legal review).
- Creators can write quest drafts. The owner publishes them.
- The journal screen is coming in Foxy's UI. The engine is built.
